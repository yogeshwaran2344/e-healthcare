Patient Trust & Privacy

Security, Cryptography & Privacy Center

Engineered to comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and Indian DPDP guidelines with verifiable mathematical security at every tier.

1. AES-256 Off-Chain Vault

All sensitive medical reports, diagnostic imaging, and clinical histories are encrypted using AES-256 GCM in isolated patient vaults. Never visible without dynamic cryptographic consent.

2. SHA-256 Blockchain Anchors

Every consultation, diagnostic test, and prescription is hashed into a SHA-256 block. Evaluators can verify tamper status in real-time without exposing confidential health records.

3. 365-Day Expiring Consent

Patient grants explicit duration permissions (1h, 24h, 7d, 365d). The system proactively notifies patients 30 days before expiration, enabling 1-click renewal or automated purge.

4. Break-Glass Emergency Audit

In life-or-death emergencies, attending trauma surgeons can invoke break-glass access. Requires mandatory justification and writes an immutable audit block alerting the patient instantly.

5. Data Minimization By Design

Adheres strictly to the Principle of Least Privilege. First responders only see allergies and blood group; paramedics receive vitals; only trauma specialists access surgical history.

6. Instant 1-Click Revocation

Patients retain sovereign ownership of their data. The "Who accessed my data?" dashboard permits instant revocation of any active grant, immediately invalidating bearer tokens.

Shannon Entropy Engine
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

Traditional symptom checkers compute static disease rankings that mislead patients with premature confidence scores. Our engine computes normalized Shannon Entropy across competing diagnoses:

H(D) = - Σ P(d_i) × log2(P(d_i))

When entropy is high (>0.5), it automatically inhibits premature diagnostic declaration, flags critical missing clinical dimensions, and activates targeted discriminative questioning.

Information Gain Optimizer
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

Rather than asking a static, bloated 20-question questionnaire, the system simulates all candidate inquiries and selects the question with maximum expected Information Gain:

IG(Q) = H(D) - Σ P(a) × H(D | Q=a)

This guarantees minimal cognitive fatigue for patients in distress while maximally splitting competing differential hypotheses.

Minimum Diagnostic Test Set
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

Formulates diagnostic testing as a multi-objective Pareto optimization problem: maximizing diagnostic information gain while minimizing patient out-of-pocket costs and physical invasiveness:

min Σ Cost(t) × x_i s.t. Σ InfoGain(t) ≥ Θ_threshold

Enforces deterministic safety overrides: any suspected cardiac, stroke, or pulmonary embolism condition triggers mandatory priority lab/ECG recommendations regardless of cost.

Explainable Decision Map
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

Opaque AI percentages are dangerous in clinical medicine. Our Explainable Health Decision Map provides bidirectional numerical factor attributions for every symptom, comorbidity, and lab parameter:

+24 (Troponin elevation) • +18 (Left arm pain) • -10 (Normal resting ECG)

Gives reviewing physicians clear visibility into exactly which clinical evidence drove the AI's risk assessment.

Doctor-AI Disagreement Engine
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

When a doctor diagnoses Condition B while the AI predicted Condition A, the system does not simply erase the AI prediction. It logs an immutable Clinical Disagreement Record capturing:

  • AI probability distribution and reasoning
  • Doctor clinical exam override rationale
  • Tests and biomarkers reviewed

This creates the necessary supervisory dataset for closed-loop machine learning calibration.

Closed-Loop Learning Loop
Technical Contribution & Novelty:

Closes the diagnostic loop through 3-way reconciliation: AI Prediction \(\leftrightarrow\) Doctor Decision \(\leftrightarrow\) Confirmed Ground Truth Outcome (from follow-up pathology, biopsy, or imaging). Automatically attributes model errors (Missing Symptom, Weighting Error, or Atypical Presentation) to continually calibrate Bayesian priors.
