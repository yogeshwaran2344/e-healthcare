Technical Architecture

Adaptive Closed-Loop Clinical Decision System

Moving beyond superficial AI chatbots to a rigorous, closed-loop diagnostic framework that evaluates uncertainty, asks targeted discriminative questions, optimizes diagnostic test sets, logs clinical divergence, and learns from verified patient outcomes.

1. Dynamic Shannon Entropy Engine

Calculates posterior probabilities across candidate conditions and computes normalized Shannon entropy (0-100% uncertainty). Prevents premature diagnostic lock-in and flags missing critical clinical dimensions.

2. Next-Best-Question Optimizer

Evaluates Information Gain \(IG(Q) = H(D) - E[H(D|Q)]\) across candidate inquiry paths. Dynamically selects the single optimal question capable of maximally splitting competing disease hypotheses.

3. Minimum Diagnostic Test Set

Multi-objective Pareto knapsack optimizer selects the minimal, safest, and most cost-effective diagnostic test combination. Enforces hard safety overrides for acute cardiac, stroke, and thrombotic emergencies.

4. Explainable Decision Map

Replaces opaque confidence scores with granular positive (+18, +24) and negative (-10) numerical feature attributions. Gives reviewing physicians clear insight into every symptom, comorbidity, and biomarker weight.

5. Clinician-AI Disagreement Engine

Detects diagnostic or triage divergence between AI and attending physicians. Stores standardized Clinical Disagreement Records categorized under atypical presentation, bedside exam override, or pre-test bias.

6. Outcome Verification & Learning Loop

Executes 3-way reconciliation (AI Prediction \(\leftrightarrow\) Doctor Decision \(\leftrightarrow\) Confirmed Outcome). Automatically categorizes failure modes and feeds continuous recalibration into prior probabilities.
